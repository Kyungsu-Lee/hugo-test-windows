{{- /* The aside panel on a post that belongs to a series: two tabs, Chapters and Contents. Chapters is the whole series in reading order, optionally broken into named groups; Contents is the page's own table of contents — the thing that used to sit here alone under "On this page". Grouping comes from a `seriesGroup` string in each part's front matter, and a heading is emitted whenever it changes as the list is walked. So groups follow reading order rather than overriding it: a part is never lifted out of sequence to join its group, which means a `seriesGroup` that jumps around produces the same heading twice. That is the honest rendering of it — the fix is the ordering, not the display. Only one tab renders when there is only one thing to show: a post with no headings worth listing gets chapters with no tab strip at all, rather than a tab that opens onto nothing. Below 760px the aside is gone and this same element is moved into the sheet from partials/panel-sheet.html — hence one panel per page, and ids rather than nth-child anywhere it is addressed. Takes a dict: page, series (the term page), toc, showToc. */ -}} {{- $page := .page -}} {{- $series := .series -}} {{- $toc := .toc -}} {{- $parts := partial "series-parts.html" $series -}} {{- $tabs := .showToc -}} {{- /* Which part this is. Used for "Part 3 of 8" under the title — the reader wants to know how much of the series is behind them, and the chapter list alone does not say it at a glance once it is long enough to scroll. */ -}} {{- $index := 0 -}} {{- range $i, $p := $parts -}} {{- if eq $p.RelPermalink $page.RelPermalink }}{{ $index = add $i 1 }}{{ end -}} {{- end -}}

It is the only series navigation left on a phone with no JavaScript, since the sheet that would otherwise carry this panel cannot open either. */ -}} {{- end }}
