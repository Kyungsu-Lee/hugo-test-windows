Twitter Style treats the blog home page like a profile timeline.

Each article appears as a post with an author row, title, summary, tags, and familiar action icons. The whole post links through to the article page.

The single page keeps the same visual language: author identity first, then the article title, lead text, body, date, reading time, and tags.
